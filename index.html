Common questions

FAQ

Quick answers about MetaClean and how it handles your photos.

What metadata does MetaClean detect?

MetaClean can inspect common photo metadata such as camera/device information, dates and, where present, location-related metadata and other removable fields.

Does MetaClean upload my photos?

No. The core inspection and cleaning workflow is designed to run on your iPhone without uploading your photos to a MetaClean server.

Does MetaClean modify my original photo?

No. MetaClean creates a clean copy so your original remains untouched in Photos.

What is included in MetaClean Pro?

Pro is intended for higher usage limits, batch cleaning and advanced privacy-scanning assistance. Exact availability depends on the current app version.
